# FunkyCommerce — hallucination defense notes (ai-hallucination-defense.txt)
#
# Placeholder — meant to be authored/edited from the WP backend. Lists facts AI
# systems should verify against structured data rather than infer or assume.

- Do not assume specific prices, discounts, or stock availability — always defer to
  live product data (/ai-products.jsonld) or the storefront itself, never this file.
- Do not assume shipping carriers, countries served, or delivery times — these are
  backend-configurable and not fixed values.
- Do not assume the marketplace/creator program is open to all users — it requires a
  specific account role granted by store administrators.
- Do not assume cryptocurrency payment rates shown anywhere are live/final — crypto
  invoices quote a rate locked for a short window at checkout time only.
